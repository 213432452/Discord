This Privacy Policy explains how voicemessag (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use Halloween Rewards Bot (“the Bot”) on Discord.

1. Information the Bot handles

Depending on which features are enabled and how the Bot is configured, it may store or process moderation records such as:

  • Warnings issued to a member.
  • Kick and ban records.
  • Moderation log entries, including the action, reason if provided, and date/time.
  • Discord identifiers needed to associate a record with a server, member, or moderator.

Discord may separately process information under its own policies. The Bot should not request or store message content or other information unless a feature genuinely needs it; this policy must be updated if the Bot does so.

2. How information is used

Moderation records are used to provide the Bot’s features, show staff previous actions, maintain server moderation history, troubleshoot problems, and protect the Bot from abuse.

3. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell moderation records. Information may be handled by the infrastructure or hosting providers used to run the Bot, where necessary to operate it, or disclosed when required by law. The owner should list any other integrations or providers that can access stored data before publishing this policy.

4. Retention and deletion requests

Moderation records should be kept only for as long as they are needed for the Bot’s moderation features, security, or legal obligations. Actual retention depends on the Bot’s implementation and configuration. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of a moderation record, contact voicemessag through the Bot’s official support/contact channel and include enough information to identify the relevant server and record. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens. Requests may be limited where records must be retained for legitimate or legal reasons.

5. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored moderation records. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Server administrators should restrict access to moderation commands and logs to trusted staff.

6. Children and Discord

The Bot is intended to be used within Discord and subject to Discord’s age requirements and rules. Do not use the Bot in a way that violates Discord’s policies or applicable law.

7. International processing

Depending on the hosting and service providers used, information may be processed in countries other than the country where a server member lives. The owner should update this section if the hosting arrangements are known and provide any disclosures required by applicable law.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Bot’s features or data practices change. The current version and its effective date will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact voicemessag through the Bot’s official support/contact channel. Before publishing, ensure users can actually reach the owner using that channel.
